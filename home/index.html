Our story

A neighborhood café for students, workers and barkadas

Brew & Bite started in 2022 as a small coffee counter near the city center. Today we have 14 tables, a full kitchen and a team of nine who know most of our regulars by name and by order.

We use Benguet arabica for our espresso, Davao cacao for our chocolate drinks and fresh milk delivered every morning. Our rice meals are cooked the way we like them at home: generous, garlicky and served with atchara on the side.
