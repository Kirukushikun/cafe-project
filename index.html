How an order moves

From the table QR to the kitchen in four steps

Scan and order

The customer scans the table QR, adds a Café Latte (L) with oat milk and an extra shot, then checks out as dine-in.

Pay

GCash or Maya marks the order Paid right away. Cash orders wait for the cashier to confirm.

Kitchen prepares

The order appears on the kitchen display. Ingredients are deducted from stock based on the recipe.

Ready

The barista taps Ready. The customer’s tracker updates and the order is served to Table 5.

GCash

Payments are simulated. Tapping Pay saves a payment record with the method, amount, status Paid, a reference number like GC-20261011-0001, and the date and time. No real money moves and no outside service is contacted. The dashboard and reports total sales from these records.
